Why Sellers Come to Us

Traditional Selling Takes Time You May Not Have

Selling through a traditional agent means months of viewings, styling your home, negotiations, and waiting for the buyer's financing. If that financing falls through after weeks of waiting — you start over again. Nobody knows what the market looks like by then.

More homeowners across the Netherlands are choosing to sell directly to wijkopen. No delays. No uncertainty. Just a clear outcome.

The average traditional home sale takes 3–6 months. Ours takes 24 hours.

Core Service

We Buy Your Home Exactly As It Is

No conditions. You do not need to renovate, clean, stage, or repair anything before selling to us. We assess the property as is — with all its quirks, maintenance issues, or outdated interiors — and make you a fair, concrete offer within 24 hours.

No financing contingency means the offer is the offer. There is no risk of the deal falling apart because a buyer's bank pulled funding.

What 'As-Is' Means in Practice

  • ✓ Outdated kitchen or bathroom — no problem
  • ✓ Deferred maintenance or structural issues
  • ✓ Leaks, fire damage, or contamination
  • ✓ Already on Funda for months with no offers
  • ✓ Real estate agents refusing to list it

Core Service

Sell Today. Stay as Long as You Need.

Once you accept our offer, you do not have to rush out the door. You can continue living in your home for up to two years after the transfer — or move as soon as two weeks from now if that suits you better.

This Is Especially Valuable If You Are:

  • ✓ Purchasing a new-build that is not yet complete
  • ✓ Searching for a suitable rental without rush
  • ✓ Going through a life transition that needs time
  • ✓ Simply not ready to leave and wanting peace of mind

You set the pace. We adapt to you — not the other way around. And if you find your next home sooner than expected, we move the transfer date forward without complications.

Divorce

A shared home needs to be divided — but you cannot simply split a house. Normally that means hiring an agent, enduring months of viewings, and trying to agree on a price with strangers — while both of you just want to move on.

We buy directly. No viewings. No drawn-out negotiations. A concrete offer within 24 hours, everything handled through the notary, and both parties receive their share.

Inheritance

You have inherited a property that nobody wants to live in. Perhaps it is outdated, has been empty for a while, or needs significant work. The estate needs to be divided and monthly costs keep running.

We buy the property as is. No renovation required, no upfront investment, no estate complications. Settlement handled through the notary so all heirs can move forward quickly.
