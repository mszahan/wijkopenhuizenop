## ━━ COLOR & DESIGN SYSTEM ━━

### WHY THESE COLORS

The palette pairs a deep, trustworthy navy with a muted sage-teal green. Both colors were chosen to feel comforting — not corporate-cold, not aggressively bright. Navy communicates stability and professionalism (important for a real estate brand dealing with high-stakes decisions). The muted green on CTAs communicates 'go ahead' and forward movement, without the harshness of a lime or neon green. Together they feel calm, grounded, and credible.

### PRIMARY COLOR — NAVY

- **Hex:** `#2C3E5D`
- **Use:** Navigation bar, footer, dark section backgrounds, headings, all primary text elements
- **Feeling:** Stable, trustworthy, professional. Reminiscent of Dutch architecture and financial solidity.
- 🎨 **COLOR:** Never use pure black (`#000000`) on this site. Always use the navy or dark text (`#1C1C2E`) instead. This keeps the brand feeling warm rather than harsh.

### CTA GREEN — SAGE TEAL

- **Hex:** `#4A7C6F` (primary CTA buttons, icons, accent borders, checkmarks)
- **Hex:** `#3D6860` (hover state — slightly darker, never neon or bright)
- **Hex:** `#E8F0EE` (very light green tint — use as subtle background on highlight cards)
- **Use:** All primary call-to-action buttons, benefit checkmarks, step number badges, active nav indicators
- **Feeling:** Growth, forward movement, reliability. Soft enough to not shout, clear enough to convert.
- 🎨 **COLOR:** The green should NEVER appear as lime (`#00FF00`) or teal (`#008080`). Keep it muted. If in doubt, add more gray to the mix. The target feeling is 'a walk in a quiet park', not 'a traffic light'.

### SUPPORTING COLORS

- **Background:** `#F4F6F9` — cool off-white, slightly blue-tinted. Pairs naturally with navy.
- **Alternate section BG:** `#EEF1F5` — one step darker, used to visually separate sections without hard borders.
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` — card backgrounds, form inputs, content areas.
- **Text (dark):** `#1C1C2E` — near-black with a slight blue undertone. Warmer than pure black.
- **Text (muted):** `#5A6475` — for secondary copy, captions, metadata, placeholder text.
- **Divider:** `#D8DCE6` — very subtle blue-gray. Use for card borders and section rules.

### BUTTON STYLING

- **Primary Button (CTA):** background `#4A7C6F`, text `#FFFFFF`, font Calibri Bold 15px, padding 14px 32px, border-radius 6px
- **Hover:** background `#3D6860`, transition 200ms ease
- **Secondary Button (outline):** border 2px solid `#2C3E5D`, text `#2C3E5D`, background transparent
- **Hover secondary:** background `#2C3E5D`, text white
- **Disabled:** background `#A8BDB9`, cursor not-allowed
- 🎨 **COLOR:** Buttons should feel substantial — not tiny. Minimum height 48px on desktop, 52px on mobile. The green should fill the button fully, not be used as an outline for the primary action.
