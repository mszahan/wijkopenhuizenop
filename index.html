We Handle Complex Situations

Selling During a Divorce or After Inheriting a Property?

We make it straightforward.

Divorce Settlement

A shared home needs to be divided — but a house is not something you can simply split. Normally this means hiring an agent, waiting months for viewings, and negotiating the sale price with strangers while both of you just want to move on.

We buy your home directly. No viewings, no staging, no months of uncertainty. Both parties receive their share, and everything is handled cleanly through the notary. A concrete offer within 24 hours.

Inheritance Settlement

You have inherited a property — perhaps outdated, perhaps empty for some time — and the estate needs to be divided. Fixed costs keep running with no income coming in.

We buy the property directly as is. No upfront investment, no estate complications, no renovation required. Everything is handled neatly through the notary so all heirs can receive their share and move forward quickly.
