The Challenge

Selling a Rented Property Through Traditional Routes Is Slow and Complicated

When a tenant has an indefinite rental contract, most property owners feel stuck. Selling with the tenant in place typically means accepting a significant rented-state discount. Waiting for the tenant to leave voluntarily can take years.

Box 3 tax regulations add another layer of pressure. With new rules coming into effect, many investors are reconsidering their portfolios. If large numbers sell around 2028, the market could become oversaturated and prices may drop. Right now, prices are still strong.

Waiting for tenants to leave can take years. We buy with the tenant in place — within 24 hours.

Why We Pay More

We Buy at Values Above the Rented-State Market Rate

Most private buyers purchasing rented property do so through Box 3 — their personal investment allocation — which comes with higher financing costs. We invest outside of Box 3, giving us lower financing costs and greater flexibility. Instead of only assessing the current rented value, we factor in the future vacant value.
