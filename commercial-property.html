The Challenge

Commercial Property Sales Are Slow, Complex, and Uncertain

Whether your commercial property is vacant or rented, selling through traditional channels is rarely straightforward. Vacant properties mean ongoing costs — mortgage, maintenance, insurance — with no income to offset them. Even when a buyer shows interest, a long chain of due diligence, financing approvals, and negotiations means months of uncertainty.

Rented commercial properties carry their own challenges. Finding a buyer willing to take on an existing commercial lease — at a price that makes sense for you — is difficult. And because most private buyers invest through Box 3, their offers are often significantly discounted.

There is a better way.
